# Power BI Dashboard Design

1. Executive Overview — KPI cards, enrollment trend, GPA by department, fee status.
2. Academic Performance — course matrix, pass-rate chart, credits-vs-grade scatter.
3. Attendance & Risk — risk segmentation, attendance trend, at-risk student table.
4. Financial Analytics — fee KPIs, waterfall, treemap, overdue-by-type.
5. Student Drill-through — student profile, GPA, attendance, fees and course history.

Recommended slicers: Department, Academic Year, Semester, Student Status, Course and Risk Classification.
